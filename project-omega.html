Project Omega

A mysterious experiment that changed everything.

Part 1 — The Beginning

Ek secret scientific project ke peeche ek aisi technology develop ki ja rahi thi jo human body ki limits ko badal sakti thi. Is project ka naam tha — Project Omega.

Laboratory ke andar scientists quantum energy aur advanced technology par kaam kar rahe the. Lekin experiment ke results unki expectations se bilkul alag nikle.

Ek taraf powerful technology create ho rahi thi, aur doosri taraf kuch log is technology ka use apne dangerous plans ke liye karna chahte the.

Isi experiment ke beech ek ordinary insaan ki zindagi hamesha ke liye badalne wali thi.

Part 2 — The Experiment

Laboratory mein Project Omega ka next phase shuru hua. Energy system activate hote hi poori facility mein alarms bajne lage.

Scientists ko samajh aa gaya ki experiment expected parameters se bahar ja raha hai.

Lekin ab bahut der ho chuki thi.
