Project Omega

A Davaat Imagine World Story

Chapter 1 — The Experiment

Ek secret laboratory mein ek mysterious experiment chal raha tha. Is experiment ka naam tha Project Omega.

Scientists ka maanna tha ki ek nayi technology insaan ki physical aur mental abilities ko dramatically badal sakti hai.

Lekin experiment ke dauraan kuch unexpected hua. Laboratory ki machines suddenly activate ho gayi aur poori facility emergency mode mein chali gayi.

Project Omega ab sirf ek scientific experiment nahi raha tha. Iske peeche ek aisa secret tha jo poori duniya ko badal sakta tha.

Aur kahani yahin se shuru hoti hai...
